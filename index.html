My AP Drawing Portfolio 2026

Sustained Investigation

“How do seemingly innocent habits and addictions affect peoples’ psyches?“ Having no experience with addiction, I began by examining the obvious, stereotypical addictions from a removed point of view (slide 1). I think most people see addiction as a “personal failing or weakness" but I wanted to show the “normalness” of it. I went from the most well-known to more obscure habitats that evolve to obsession (3, 8, 9, 5) while compositionally ‘being in the place of the addict.” Later in (11, 13, 12) I explored watching the person in their world (15). I wanted to convey addiction with care.

Sustained Investigation

"While experimenting and revising addict viewpoint and placement in my compositions, I pushed myself to make digital art outside of cartoons & graphic design. Notably, with tools I’d never used before: unique texture brushes (2, 3, 5, 13), G`Mic filters (5, 9, 15), & gradient maps (3). Coupled with my atypical study of new genres, I paid homage to concept art from Omori, Zelda, & Minecraft, with my varied painterly experiments, using them to reflect the psyche, habits, highs & more. Finally, this led to me revising & rerendering multiple pieces; scrapping & reutilizing ideas throughout."
